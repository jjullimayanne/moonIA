# Bibliography

* **Silburt, A., Ali-Dib, M., Zhu, C., Jackson, A., Valencia, D., Kissin, Y., Tamayo, D., Menou, K. (2019).** Lunar crater identification via deep learning. *Icarus* 317, 27–38. doi:10.1016/j.icarus.2018.06.022. arXiv:1803.02192. Code: github.com/silburt/DeepMoon (MIT). Data: doi:10.5281/zenodo.1133969 (CC-BY-SA-4.0).
* **Yang, C., Zhao, H., Bruzzone, L., Benediktsson, J. A., Liang, Y., Liu, B. et al. (2020).** Lunar impact crater identification and age estimation with Chang'E data by deep and transfer learning. *Nature Communications* 11, 6358. doi:10.1038/s41467-020-20215-y. Data: doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.12768539 (CC BY 4.0). Code/data: github.com/hszhaohs/DeepCraters.
* **Head, J. W. et al. (2010).** Global distribution of large lunar craters: implications for resurfacing and impactor populations. *Science* 329, 1504–1507. doi:10.1126/science.1195050.
* **Povilaitis, R. Z. et al. (2018).** Crater density differences: exploring regional resurfacing, secondary crater populations, and crater saturation equilibrium on the Moon. *Planetary and Space Science* 162, 41–51. doi:10.1016/j.pss.2017.05.006.
* **Barker, M. K. et al. (2016).** A new lunar digital elevation model from the Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter and SELENE Terrain Camera. *Icarus* 273, 346–355. doi:10.1016/j.icarus.2015.07.039.
* **Ronneberger, O., Fischer, P., Brox, T. (2015).** U-Net: convolutional networks for biomedical image segmentation. MICCAI. arXiv:1505.04597.
* **Horn, B. K. P. (1981).** Hill shading and the reflectance map. *Proceedings of the IEEE* 69(1), 14–47.
* **Crater Analysis Techniques Working Group (1979).** Standard techniques for presentation and analysis of crater size-frequency data. *Icarus* 37, 467–474 (√2 binning).
* **Archinal, B. A. et al. (2018).** Report of the IAU Working Group on Cartographic Coordinates and Rotational Elements: 2015. *Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy* 130, 22.
* **FTL Brazil — Space Tech Bootcamp 2026**, e-book: https://ftl-brazil-2026.github.io/ebook/ (CRS & projections, DEM, U-Net and training pipeline chapters).
